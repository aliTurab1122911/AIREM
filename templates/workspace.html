Linguistic rewrite pipeline

No ML models are used by this rewriter. The engine first isolates protected facts and terminology, then applies V12-inspired lexical and sentence-structure transformations, and finally compresses the result back toward the source word count.

Always protected: numerical data, percentages, money, dates, citations, quotations, URLs/emails, equations/code identifiers, file names, units/measurements, acronyms, likely names/organisations and automatically detected repeated technical terms.

Use this field for any course-specific phrase, model name, legal term, product name or technical expression that must remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The detection score shown elsewhere in AIREM is diagnostic only. It is not used to generate, rank, accept or roll back a rewrite.
